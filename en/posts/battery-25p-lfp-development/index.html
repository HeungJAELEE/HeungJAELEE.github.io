25P LFP Cell Development and Trial Production

A new-cell case that traced electrolyte, Clad Tab, and inspection changes through internal sample gates, about 20,000-cell trial production, and customer qualification

Project period
Jan 2025 A sample · Feb 2025 B/C samples and trial production · Mar 2025 customer qualification
Context
Kumyang Battery Technology Team · LFP power-cell development for an overseas customer request
Scope
Process-condition decisions, trials, sample building, semi-finished inspection, and standards documentation in joint development

Field problem and result summary

Field problem
Material and structure changes in the LFP power cell made existing 2170 assembly and inspection settings unsuitable.
What I owned
Working with development, I separated change effects by process and owned assembly-wide sample building, inspection, condition decisions, and control-document revisions.
Verified result
The team advanced through internal A, B, and C samples, about 20,000-cell initial trial production, customer qualification, and CCB P.O approval. Commercial production did not proceed because of pricing.
Initial trial production About 20,000 cells
Crimping design decision Adjusted for equipment and life constraints

Development flow

A foreign customer requested an LFP power cell. Material and geometry changes made the existing 2170 conditions unsuitable, so process CTQs and validation methods were rebuilt from A Sample onward.

  1. 2025.01A SampleDefined change scope and process CTQs.
  2. 2025.02B/C SampleReset impregnation, welding, inspection, and downstream conditions.
  3. 2025.02Trial productionChecked the connected process flow in about 20,000 cells.
  4. 2025.03Customer qualificationReceived production approval and completed handoff documentation.

01

Process-condition validation

Electrolyte impregnation

Actual impregnation was verified through cell disassembly instead of relying on design fill quantity.

Signal and action

  • The initial sample showed an unimpregnated region despite using the designed fill amount.
  • Reset vacuum, hold time, pressure, pressure hold, and repeat count.
  • Measured the unimpregnated width directly after disassembly.
  • Reviewed weight, capacity, IR, and life.

Result and control

  • Reduced the directly measured unimpregnated width from about 6 mm toward about 1.5 mm.
  • Development approved the result and failure analysis.
  • Revised recipe checklists, QCP, and internal process standards.
02

Change-based welding qualification

Clad Tab resistance welding

Welding conditions and inspection criteria were rebuilt for the new material and Embo.

Change and validation

  • The new Clad Tab material and Embo made legacy conditions unsuitable.
  • Rechecked pressure, voltage, time, and electrode diameter.
  • Verified nugget size, tensile strength, and fracture shape.

Result boundary

  • Prepared process and inspection criteria for production handoff.
  • Detailed recipes and internal specifications are not published.
03

Inspection-system validation

X-Ray and Vision decisions

Product abnormalities were separated from ROI, count, and reflectivity effects.

X-Ray

  • Turn-count changes required new Count and ROI conditions.
  • Disassembled, sectioned, and microscopically reviewed rejected cells.
  • Changed Count and ROI only after confirming no actual product abnormality and receiving approval.

Vision

  • New Clad Tab geometry and reflectivity required revised settings.
  • Adjusted settings while maintaining production.
  • Monitored four production batches on site.

Control

  • Kept product-change and inspection-system-change decisions separate.
  • Reflected approved decision conditions in inspection and process documents.

Result and stage

  • Produced about 20,000 trial cells.
  • Completed customer qualification and received production approval in March 2025.
  • Prepared process standards, QCP, P-FMEA, and inspection criteria for production handoff.
  • Commercial mass production and long-term stability are separate scopes.
  • A trial-production yield recalled from memory is excluded from confirmed KPIs and card outcomes.
