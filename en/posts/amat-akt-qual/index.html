Manufacturing case study

Technical qualification

Technical Qualification of CVD and PECVD Components

Aligning same-sample measurement results before documenting approved process and inspection criteria

Project period
Qualification work during YMC employment
Context
Completed in YMC's Technical R&D Center
Scope
Process and inspection specification rebuild, measurement correlation, sample validation, and technical audit support

Field problem and result summary

Field problem
Stable internal results were insufficient when customer measurement methods and acceptance criteria differed.
What I owned
I rebuilt surface-treatment and inspection criteria, including Hi-Pot, acid resistance, and corrosion resistance, and supported same-sample correlation and technical audits.
Verified result
Repeated same-sample measurements identified differences in internal and customer equipment and decision methods, and the technical-qualification conditions were added to QCPs, run sheets, and inspection standards.

Why an internal pass was not enough

The same sample could produce different results when internal and customer equipment, measurement locations, and decision methods differed. Instead of changing the process recipe first, measurement differences were separated from actual process differences using the same samples.

Scope I owned

I rebuilt surface-treatment, Hi-Pot, acid-resistance, and corrosion-resistance inspection conditions for customer requirements and handled same-sample comparison, corrective samples, technical-audit response, and approved-condition documentation.

How internal and customer measurement differences were aligned

Item alignedDifference checkedDocumentation
Sample conditionMaterial, surface, and pretreatmentCompare the same sample under the same pretreatment
EquipmentBias and repeat difference between internal and customer toolsPrevent equipment difference from being misread as a process issue
Measurement methodLocation, repeat count, order, and readingFix inspection method and location
Customer requirementEnglish specification, process condition, and inspection itemConnect QCP, run sheet, and inspection standard

Representative items such as thickness, roughness, and withstand voltage were measured repeatedly to identify equipment and method differences. Without a public statistical result, the case states that differences were checked and inspection conditions aligned rather than claiming that correlation was fully established.

Corrective samples and technical audits

Technical-audit findings were separated into process, inspection, and documentation items. Owners, actions, and retests were connected, while unverified items remained corrective-sample work instead of being passed by assumption.

Technical and business results

AreaResult
TechnicalProcess, inspection, and decision conditions for CVD/PECVD components were documented for technical qualification
BusinessContinued trading was a separate company decision that also considered profitability
