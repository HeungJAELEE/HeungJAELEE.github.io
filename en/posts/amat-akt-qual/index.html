CVD and PECVD Component Process Reconfiguration and Technical Qualification

I aligned etching, bead blasting, oxalic anodizing, and Hi-Pot inspection with the customer specification. The revised test method closed the audit finding after seven readings and three retests

Project period
Dec 2017–Dec 2018
Context
Completed in YMC's Technical R&D Center
Scope
English specifications and CTQs, Process Flow and Run Sheets, process changes, counterpart samples, measurement, external analysis, and closing the audit finding

Manufacturing project summary

Problem definition
Gap between legacy process/inspection conditions and product-family specifications and Hi-Pot disposition
Key role
English specification→Process Flow and Run Sheet conversion, process-level 5M, and Hi-Pot correlation
Key result
One audit finding closed through seven readings and three retests; technical qualification and about one year of follow-on supply

4. Hi-Pot measurement correlation

Counterpart samples from the same bath and manufacturing condition
→ customer-designated measurement locations
→ seven repeated readings per sample
→ internal/customer equipment comparison
→ ramp time and test-sequence alignment
→ Arc Counter disposition alignment
→ revised counterpart samples
→ three retests under one decision basis
→ closure of one technical-audit finding

Equipment, location, repeat, and sequence differences were removed before changing the process recipe. The three retests then established correlation between internal inspection and customer disposition.
