Quality problem the team addressed

The team prioritized the risk of passing a real defect as normal rather than overall accuracy alone. At the same time, an overly low decision threshold would increase good-part reinspection and production burden.

Team system

The team demonstrated the path from image input to model output and manual-review UI. I led overall planning and owned modeling, evaluation, and missed-defect-first decision criteria. The report was joint work and the team lead presented.

Changes observed on separately captured samples

Public welding material was used to train candidate models. Separately captured samples were used only to inspect how errors changed under different lighting, angle, and surface reflection. They were not an independent test set or production-performance validation. Because no dedicated public repository, fixed evaluation set, or reproducible runtime is available, data counts and final performance scores are excluded from headline results.

Team result and current stage

The five-person team completed a training PoC that compared error behavior under different capture conditions and reviewed missed defects together with reinspection burden. Production use would still require an independent set separated by product, lot, and capture time; fixed camera conditions; inference-time checks; shadow testing; and model and decision-criteria change history.
