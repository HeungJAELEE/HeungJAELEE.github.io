Project background and acceptance challenge

Supplier FAT confirmed limited samples, individual functions, and auto cycles. After site installation, however, process handoffs, semi-finished tolerances, operator flow, inspection results, and production history had to work together. A supplier function-completion notice was therefore insufficient to start pilot production.

I applied one acceptance basis to 11 core machines, 13 major production processes, and a separate EOL height-inspection unit. My direct scope covered specifications and acceptance criteria, FAT/SAT inspection, punch disposition and retest, real-cell process and measurement checks, initial planning/WIP/lot/defect-history operation, and standard handoff.

FAT/SAT inspection and punch closure

The FAT inspection structure was reused during site SAT. Installation, process handoffs, operator movement, semi-finished tolerances, and actual-cell assembly, welding, leak, and electrical results were connected so that machine function and product results were dispositioned together.

Symptom → quality impact → owner and due date → action → same-basis retest → actual-cell result → close or reopen
StageDecision questionRequired evidence
Fix the symptomWhich motion, sensor, or quality signal repeatsError location, run record, and sample result
Link quality impactHow the machine issue affects semi-finished or cell resultsGeometry, welding, leak, and electrical checks
Assign actionWho changes what, by when, and under which retest conditionOwner, due date, action, and retest method
Retest on one basisWhether pre/post correction uses the same inspection and product criteriaFAT/SAT sheet and reference sample
ClosureWhether function, safety, product quality, and process link pass togetherActual-cell result and punch history
ReopenWhether another condition or lot reproduces the issueRecurrence record, added cause, and follow-up action

Supplier completion statements did not close a punch. Closure required the same inspection and product criteria to pass again and agree with the actual-cell result.

Approximately 1,000-cell pilot run

After production-critical punches were closed, approximately 1,000 cells were operated over about five days to check the process, inspection, and production-history flow. Daily planning and process priority were adjusted while material, WIP, lot, equipment, defect, and rework history were connected in the production record.
