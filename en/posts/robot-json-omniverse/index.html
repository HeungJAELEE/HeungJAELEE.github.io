Indy7 Automotive Factory Control and Traceability Digital Twin PoC

A product-level flow connecting QR serial traceability, three Indy7 assembly/loading stations, YOLO inspection, PLC/SCADA state, and MySQL history. I led overall planning and directly owned robot control and process integration.

What an interviewer should confirm in 30 seconds

Problem
Product identity, robot completion, inspection decisions, and loading history were disconnected.
What I led
Project objective, process scenario, system structure, and cross-team integration order.
Hands-on scope
Indy7 control, station command/state integration, and integrated-sequence validation.
Team boundary
Detailed vision, Ladder, and SCADA/database work by teammates; report and presentation by the team lead.
Verified output
QR → body/window assembly → vision → good loading/defect pass physical demonstration.
Later extension
PC-HMI, Conty JSON, IndyDCP, MQTT, DB Worker, and read-only Web Twin.
Role boundary: I was not the team lead or sole developer. I led the project problem, process flow, and system integration order and directly owned robot control and integration.
