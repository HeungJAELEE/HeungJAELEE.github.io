    Case A. Mixed-acid anodizing for an OLED dry-etch upper electrode

    1. Legacy-process issue and CTQs

    The higher OLED process temperature produced concurrent failures in the legacy mixed-acid film:

    • cracks and appearance degradation after heat exposure;
    • particle increase and etch-rate loss;
    • Hi-Pot variation relative to film thickness; and
    • position-dependent variation between small samples and large-area parts.

    Success criteria

    • crack control after thermal exposure at the customer-required thickness;
    • aligned thickness and Hi-Pot disposition on the same sample;
    • reproduction of the 100×100 mm condition at 500×500 mm; and
    • customer qualification followed by product application.

    Measurement and environment checks

    • Measurement: Thickness, cracks, appearance, and Hi-Pot variation · Approval evidence · Same-sample repeated measurement and aligned disposition
    • Environment: Customer high-temperature use and internal air test · Separated development condition · Independent use-environment and test-environment controls

    4. Condition development and large-area scale-up

    Baseline defect history and legacy conditions
→ film, thickness, and post-treatment samples
→ 24-hour thermal exposure in air
→ at least three repeats under the same condition
→ crack, appearance, and Hi-Pot disposition
→ 100×100 mm sample
→ 500×500 mm large-area sample
→ customer qualification
→ product application

    The small-sample condition was not transferred directly. Current distribution, racking, and position-dependent film variation were revalidated on the large-area sample.

    50 μm Hi-Pot comparisonAbout 2.4 kVAbout 2.7 kVImproved electrical direction

    6. Results

    • Redesigned mixed-acid film condition, thickness, racking, and inspection gates
    • Completed 100×100 mm→500×500 mm scale-up validation
    • Obtained customer qualification and product application
    • Tracked stabilization for about one year after development
    • Revised run sheets, test results, change reports, process standards, and inspection criteria
