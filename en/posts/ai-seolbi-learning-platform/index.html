Learner problem

Exam questions, answers, theory, and image rights come from different sources. Fluent completion of unsupported gaps can create incorrect learning material.

Actual learner flow

  1. Choose the required written, descriptive, or practical path.
  2. Before submission, view the question and necessary material without answers or explanations.
  3. After submission, receive the answer, explanation, and option feedback.
  4. Return to linked theory and sources for missed items.
  5. Keep answer, rights, or image conflicts off the public surface on HOLD.

Public versus held material

flowchart LR A["Question, theory, and source"] --> B["Normalize ID, type, and theory link"] --> C{"Answer evidence?"} C -- "Conflict" --> H["HOLD"] C -- "Confirmed" --> D{"Rights and image?"} D -- "Unclear" --> H D -- "Confirmed" --> E["PUBLIC"] --> F["Answer"] --> G["Post-submit feedback and theory review"]

Answers are excluded from pre-submission delivery, not merely hidden by the interface.
