Alternative Can Supplier Pilot Transition

A supplier-transition case that translated material and geometry differences into process risks and verified quality and safety across three lots

Project period
Validated during Kumyang employment · pilot supply transition in Mar 2025
Context
Kumyang Battery Technology Team · alternative supplier validation for a cylindrical-cell component
Scope
Change review, assembly-condition reset, three-lot process/quality/safety testing, and process and approval documentation

Field problem and result summary

Field problem
The incumbent supplier could not cover the planned follow-on volume alone, so a new can supplier required manufacturing and quality validation.
What I owned
I translated material-property and bottom-geometry differences into beading, crimping, and sealing risks, then set the assembly conditions and validation sequence.
Verified result
After 600 cells across three lots and an official UN38.3 pass, purchasing and subsequent pilot production moved to the alternative can after existing stock was consumed.
Validation scope 600 cells · 3 lots
Safety verification Official UN38.3 pass

Why an alternate supplier was needed

The existing can supplier could not cover the expected follow-on demand, so an alternate source was evaluated. Differences in alloy, bottom thickness, and tolerances meant that dimensional acceptance alone was insufficient. Assembly geometry, sealing, vent behavior, and cell-level safety were checked before the pilot supply transition was approved.

Direct work and retesting

The candidate can was evaluated through assembly conditions and cell safety rather than component inspection alone. After resetting beading-knife height, depth, and pressure plus crimping and sizing pressure and height, 200 cells per lot were built across three lots, 600 cells in total.

Total height, inner diameter, appearance, sealing, and welds were compared, followed by weld sectioning, cell teardown, drop, vent burst pressure, top/bottom compression, and official UN38.3 testing.

Result and current scope

After the official UN38.3 pass, follow-on pilot production and purchasing moved to the alternative can when existing stock was consumed. Commercial production did not start because of company financing, so realized savings and long-run high-volume results were not measured.
