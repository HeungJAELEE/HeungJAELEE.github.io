User problem

The guide organizes separation reason, insured period, documents, and individual notice, then directs users to official determinations through Employment24 or the responsible employment center.

Actual user flow

  1. Find a question type close to the user’s situation.
  2. Review facts such as separation reason, period, documents, and job-search activity.
  3. Move to linked Work24, Ministry of Employment and Labor, and legal sources.
  4. Confirm differences against individual notices with the local employment center.
  5. The site does not determine personal eligibility or payment.

How a question becomes a guide page

flowchart LR A["Original question"] --> B["De-identification"] --> C["Eight-type classification"] --> D["Facts to check"] --> E["Official sources"] --> F["Q&A, guides, and search pages"] --> G["Static generation"]

Scope I built directly

  • Classified 165 de-identified questions into eight types
  • Connected official sources, facts to check, and caution text
  • Generated Q&A, guides, search pages, and structured data from one source
  • Reviewed wording that could appear to make a personal decision and added change warnings

Current operating scope

The public snapshot dated July 19, 2026 confirms 165 de-identified questions, eight types, nine practical guides, and 21 search pages. Current individual notices, Work24, local employment centers, and law take precedence over this site.
