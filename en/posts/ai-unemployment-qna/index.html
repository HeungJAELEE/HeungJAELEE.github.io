Practical Guide to Unemployment-Benefit Questions

A personal AI static service connecting 165 de-identified questions to eight categories, official sources, practical guides, and structured data

Problem

A short question such as “Can I receive unemployment benefit after resigning?” may depend on separation reason, insured periods, documents, job-search activity, and agency review. A one-sentence eligibility decision can conflict with current policy or the actual administrative disposition. Publishing the source conversations would also expose personal context and repeat many similar questions.

The project is not an eligibility chatbot. It is a static information service that guides de-identified questions toward official confirmation paths.

Representative user scene

A person who has left a job selects the type closest to their situation. The guide identifies facts to check first—such as separation reason, insured period, documents, and job-search activity—then links to the relevant Work24, Ministry of Employment and Labor, and statute sources. The user confirms the final position through an individual agency notice, current Work24 guidance, and the competent employment center rather than relying on the site’s wording.

No raw chat, name, contact information, employer, or case detail appears on the site.

What a first-time visitor sees

The home page does not start with a chatbot prompt. It first shows what the visitor should check today, then offers eight situation categories and links to practical guides. This keeps a person from treating one generated sentence as an eligibility decision.

Selecting a guide opens a sequence of facts, documents, and official channels to confirm. A visitor can therefore understand both the next action and the limit of the guide without knowing the data model behind it.

What the tool does—and does not do

The tool groups 165 de-identified questions into eight types, then connects each type to checks, official sources, nine practical guides, and 21 search pages. Users can return to the same evidence and caveats instead of receiving an unconstrained free-form answer.

The tool does not decide individual eligibility, payment, or appeal outcomes. It does not submit applications or access a Work24 account. It is not automatically current after a policy change. Individual notices, Work24, and the competent employment center always take priority over the site.

Data, AI, and human roles

The data separates question type, situation, facts and documents to check, official source, and caveat. Q&A, guides, search pages, and structured data come from one source rather than separately maintained copies.

AI assisted with taxonomy drafts, duplicate and search clusters, answer structures, and metadata. A human de-identified the source questions, checked Work24, Ministry of Employment and Labor, and statute links, and reviewed wording that could be read as an individual decision.

Screen, data, and repository structure

The service does not send a visitor’s question to an LLM at runtime. Reviewed source data is converted into static HTML ahead of publication, so the same caveat and official source appear consistently on the category, question, guide, and search pages.

The source repository is not published separately; this map shows only the responsibility units that are safe to disclose.

Build sequence

  1. Remove personal and case details and de-identify 165 source questions.
  2. Group questions with similar confirmation needs into eight types.
  3. Attach checks such as separation reason, period, documents, and job-search activity.
  4. Connect each type to Work24, Ministry of Employment and Labor, statute sources, and an official next step.
  5. Statically generate the Q&A, nine practical guides, 21 search pages, Schema.org data, and sitemap.
  6. Put content hashes on CSS and JavaScript assets and read back representative routes through Cloudflare Pages and Sites.

Company value and limitations

The same structure can support public information or employee-support programs. Grouping repeated questions and placing official evidence and a next confirmation point beside each answer makes the review boundary clearer than in an unconstrained chatbot. De-identification and raw-conversation exclusion provide a practical privacy boundary. Static generation also keeps shared evidence and caveats consistent across pages.

Administrative judgment and freshness remain the limits. Raw chats and personal notices are not published, and the site does not decide eligibility, payment, or disposition. Individual agency notices, current Work24 guidance, the competent employment center, and current statutes take priority. Unsupported conclusions and change-sensitive figures remain unpublished.

How AI and human roles were divided

AI role

Human role

External effect
Only static information sites are deployed. The service does not submit claims, decide eligibility, access Work24 accounts, or perform administrative action.

Scope and boundaries

In scope

  • 165 de-identified questions, eight categories, and practical guides
  • Work24, Ministry of Employment and Labor, and statute sources
  • Static generation, search pages, Schema.org, and hash-based cache busting

Out of scope or unverified

  • Determining individual eligibility, payment, or administrative disposition
  • Publishing raw chats, personal notices, contacts, or case details
  • Placing site wording above current statutes or agency guidance

Implementation and verification

What had to work

  • Classify each question by type, situation, checks, and official source.
  • Provide conditions requiring individual confirmation and an official next step.
  • Generate pages, search, and structured data from one source.

Quality and operating rules

  • Separate official source from explanation and mark change risk.
  • Block personal data, raw conversations, and private repository URLs.
  • Put content hashes in asset URLs to invalidate stale CSS and JavaScript.

Why it was designed this way

  1. Use typed static Q&A instead of free-form chat

    The same source, caveat, and next action can be repeatedly validated and indexed.

  2. Pair evidence with an official action path

    Users should verify personal notices and agency guidance rather than infer eligibility from an article.

  3. Control deployment caches with asset hashes

    Content and behavior must not drift because old CSS or JavaScript remains cached.
