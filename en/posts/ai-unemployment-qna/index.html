Why I built it

A short question such as “Can I qualify after resigning?” may depend on separation reason, insured periods, documents, job-search activity, an individual notice, and agency review. A website that decides the case can conflict with current law or the actual disposition.

The service is therefore a static verification aid, not an eligibility chatbot. It helps a user find:

  • the closest question type;
  • facts and documents to check;
  • the relevant Work24, ministry, or statute source;
  • what to confirm through an individual notice or competent employment center.

Why 165 questions become eight categories

Publishing raw conversations would expose personal context and create duplicates. One giant FAQ would make discovery difficult.

The pipeline de-identifies 165 questions, groups them into eight categories by intent and verification requirement, and connects repeated questions to guides and search terms. The completed snapshot includes nine practical guides and 21 search pages.

Architecture and data flow

Q&A, guide HTML, search pages, structured data, and link checks come from one source rather than separate manual copies.

Actual user verification flow

  1. The user finds the closest question type.
  2. The page lists the facts to check, such as separation reason, period, documents, and job-search activity.
  3. The user follows the linked Work24, ministry, or statute source.
  4. If the personal notice and the general guide differ, the competent employment center is the verification route.
  5. The site does not automatically decide eligibility or payment.

What I implemented directly and what AI assisted

AI assisted taxonomy, duplicate and search clusters, answer structure, and metadata drafts. It did not own source selection or case-specific legal application.

A human de-identified source conversations, checked Work24, Ministry of Employment and Labor, and statute links, and reviewed wording that could be read as an individual decision.

Normal guidance and policy-change flow

The normal path generates a static page from the same source that holds the de-identified question, checks, and official references. A broken official link or policy change does not make an old sentence a current decision automatically. Decision-like wording returns to review and the user is routed to the latest official channel.

How to use the guide

  • Start with the closest question type, then check the listed facts and documents.
  • Follow the linked Work24, ministry, or statute source for the current rule.
  • For an individual case, the personal notice and competent employment center take priority over the guide.
  • Source-sensitive conclusions are reviewed before they are regenerated.
