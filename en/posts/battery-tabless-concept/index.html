2170 Tabless A-Sample Build Validation

An exploratory A-sample case that used existing 4695 equipment and manual processes to secure functional samples and define the gates before repeat production

Project period
Jan 2025 · 2170 Tabless A-sample structure build and basic performance checks
Context
Kumyang Battery Technology Team · manufacturability review of a new cell structure
Scope
Owned process conditions, temporary jigs, sample building, welding, assembly, inspection, and evaluation; development owned structure design and documents

Field problem and result summary

Field problem
A collector-based Tabless structure differed from the lead-tab process and could not be repeated without dedicated jigs and inspection methods.
What I owned
I built temporary jigs for 4695 equipment and handled process conditions, manual assembly, welding, inspection, and performance evaluation.
Verified result
Five samples suitable for functional evaluation were secured from about 100 exploratory builds. Repeat production, manufacturability, and statistical superiority were not verified at this A-sample stage.
A-sample exploration scope About 100 exploratory builds · 5 functional samples

What this stage was meant to confirm

The A-sample stage focused on creating a build path for the tabless structure with existing 4695 equipment and temporary jigs and on securing samples suitable for functional evaluation. Laser-welding failures, internal beam entry, shorts, and assembly damage were classified so the next pilot stage could define its jig and inspection conditions.

Temporary jigs and build sequence

  • Temporary jigs were prepared for J/R position, collector alignment, and laser welding.
  • Can insertion and part of the assembly were manual.

Trial and functional checks

About 100 builds were attempted with temporary jigs and existing 4695 equipment. Laser-weld failure, internal beam intrusion, shorts, and assembly damage were separated, leaving five samples suitable for functional evaluation. The functional-evaluation entry rate was about 5% of build attempts, but this is an exploratory selection ratio, not production yield.

The five selected samples proceeded through Hi-Pot, IR, formation, charge, and capacity checks. The same materials and cycling conditions were used to compare structural direction and define inputs for dedicated jigs, repeat production, and the next pilot gate.

Current stage and next conditions

This stage confirmed build feasibility and a limited functional direction. The next step is to validate dedicated jigs and equipment, repeat-production variation, internal laser entry and short risk, productivity, and the pilot gate in sequence.
