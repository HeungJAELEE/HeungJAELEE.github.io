A-sample concept validation

2170 Tabless A-Sample Build Validation

An exploratory A-sample case that used existing 4695 equipment and manual processes to secure functional samples and define the gates before repeat production

Project period
Jan 2025 · 2170 Tabless A-sample structure build and basic performance checks
Context
Kumyang Battery Technology Team · manufacturability review of a new cell structure
Scope
Owned process conditions, temporary jigs, sample building, welding, assembly, inspection, and evaluation; development owned structure design and documents

Field problem and result summary

Field problem
A collector-based Tabless structure differed from the lead-tab process and could not be repeated without dedicated jigs and inspection methods.
What I owned
I built temporary jigs for 4695 equipment and handled process conditions, manual assembly, welding, inspection, and performance evaluation.
Verified result
After about 100 exploratory builds, five samples were suitable for functional checks; a limited same-material, same-cycle comparison indicated lower IR and improved life direction.
A-sample exploration scope About 100 exploratory builds · 5 functional samples

Validation flow

The goal was not to establish production conditions. It was to determine whether a Tabless structure could be physically built with existing 4695 equipment and temporary jigs.

01

Prototype Build–Test–Learn

Tabless structure feasibility

Functional-sample success was kept separate from repeat-production and manufacturability validation.

Build path

  • Built temporary jigs for J/R holding, Collector alignment, and Laser Welding.
  • Defined a manual assembly and welding sequence.
  • Inserted the assembly into the Can manually.

4M failure map

4MRisk reviewedDecision
ManManual holding, alignment, and weldingHigh operator dependence remained outside repeat-production scope.
MachineExisting 4695 equipment and no dedicated jigTemporary jigs were used only for feasibility.
MaterialSame materials with a new collector structureFocused the comparison on structural function.
MethodLaser weld, internal beam, Hi-Pot, short, and assembly damageLogged failures and selected samples suitable for functional tests.

Trial and functional results

  • Attempted about 100 builds.
  • The main failure order was Laser Welding, internal beam entry, Hi-Pot and short, then assembly damage.
  • Secured five samples for functional evaluation.
  • Checked Hi-Pot, IR, Formation, charging, and capacity.
  • Compared IR and life direction with same-material Lead Tab cells.

Stage

  • Confirmed build feasibility and limited functional direction.
  • Did not validate repeat production, pilot readiness, dedicated equipment, long-term reliability, or mass manufacturability.
  • Closed at the A-Sample concept-validation stage.

Next-stage Punch items

  • Dedicated jigs and equipment
  • Repeat-production capability
  • Inspection method and operator variation
  • Internal laser-entry and short safety
  • Process conditions and manual dependence
  • Productivity and pilot gates
