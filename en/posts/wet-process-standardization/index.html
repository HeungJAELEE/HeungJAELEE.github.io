Why the work was separated into three cases

At YMC’s technical R&D center, I worked on anodizing, etching, cleaning, bead blasting, bath analysis, measurement comparison, and customer qualification. Combining production development, technical qualification, and failure in one article blurred each case’s decision logic and result, so they are separated here.
