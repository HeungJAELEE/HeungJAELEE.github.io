4695 Production Transition Validation

A production-transition case that restored automatic cycles and measurement controls, then used a final one-way run to decide whether limited production could begin

Project period
4695 assembly-line limited-production validation during Kumyang employment
Context
Kumyang Battery Technology Team · production-readiness recovery for a development-complete product
Scope
Set assembly-wide trial, measurement, and setup directions while coordinating production, quality, and maintenance actions and retests

Field problem and result summary

Field problem
Development was complete, but no equipment could run normally from start to finish, so production-start conditions were rechecked from an initial SAT-level baseline.
What I owned
I set the assembly trial direction and coordinated sensor, sequence, speed, alignment, punch-list actions, and retests across engineering, maintenance, and manufacturing.
Verified result
A representative automatic cycle was restored, and two 100-cell lots plus the final 50 cells were used as production-start evidence. Long-term production stability was separate.
Automatic-cycle recovery trials 100 cells × 2 lots
Final production-start evidence 50 cells

Why we returned to initial SAT-level checks

Product development was complete, but no equipment could maintain a normal automatic cycle from the start command through completion. Operators moved each unit manually, and normal MES process history was not recorded. The line was therefore returned to an initial SAT-level review, with the laser-welding equipment joining the J/R uncoated area to the collector used as the representative recovery case.

Production-transition timeline and gates

  1. BaselineManual productionAutomatic cycles, vision decisions, and MES history did not match the actual production state.
  2. Re-SATFunction and process recheckSensors, interlocks, sequence, speed, alignment, and decision criteria were reviewed again.
  3. RecoveryRepresentative automatic cycleSafety interlocks were retained while product sensing, sequence, waiting, and alignment were normalized.
  4. Retest100 cells × 2 lotsActions were controlled through a punch list and retested under the same criteria.
  5. DecisionFinal 50 cellsThe run supported the decision to begin limited production.
  6. ControlDocuments and MESWork, inspection, punch, and production-history controls were revised or created.

The production-start gate followed automatic cycle from start to finish → normal vision and inspection → reliable measurement and disposition → production-history recording → engineering disposition for off-standard products.

Representative process recovery

Laser-welding automatic-cycle recovery

Product sensing, sequence, alignment, and speed were corrected while safety interlocks remained in place.

Initial condition

  • Operators moved each unit manually and normal MES process history was not recorded.
  • Automatic flow and quality criteria had to be rechecked from an initial SAT-level baseline.

Safety retained

  • Door, laser, and operator-protection safety interlocks were not changed.
  • Only wait time related to product sensing and process sequence was adjusted to reduce tack-time congestion.

Direct action and collaboration

  • Engineering and maintenance revalidated sensors, interlocks, sequence, speed, and alignment.
  • Manufacturing supported trials and production.

Issue control and retesting

Control itemMethod
Symptom and impactQuality, production, and safety impacts were separated.
Owner and due dateEngineering, maintenance, and manufacturing actions were connected.
RetestThe same criteria were checked again in the next lot.
ClosureItems were closed by actual operation and cell results, not by a completion reply alone.
  • Two lots of 100 cells were used after automatic-cycle recovery.
  • The final 50-cell result was used as production-start evidence.
  • The final 50 cells supported the limited-production start decision and were not treated as long-run repeat-production performance.
  • Initial production records mixed inspection-sample and process losses, so they are used only to explain the operating condition rather than as a headline yield.
