Manufacturing case study

Limited-production validation

4695 Production Transition Validation

A production-transition case that restored automatic cycles and measurement controls, then used a final one-way run to decide whether limited production could begin

Project period
4695 assembly-line limited-production validation during Kumyang employment
Context
Kumyang Battery Technology Team · production-readiness recovery for a development-complete product
Scope
Set assembly-wide trial, measurement, and setup directions while coordinating production, quality, and maintenance actions and retests

Field problem and result summary

Field problem
Development and handover were complete, but automatic cycles and vision inspection did not run normally, operators moved each unit manually, and MES history was not recorded correctly.
What I owned
I set assembly-wide trial and decision criteria, coordinated technology, maintenance, and production actions, and revised measurement methods and control documents.
Verified result
After recovery trials covering about 200 cells in two lots over roughly two weeks, the final 50-cell one-way run achieved FYD of at least 90%, which was used as the limited-production start gate.
Automatic-cycle recovery trials About 200 cells · 2 lots
Final one-way run 50 cells · FYD at least 90%

Transition flow

Product development was complete, but equipment could not maintain Auto Cycle from start to finish. Operators manually moved each unit, and normal MES history was not produced. I returned the assembly processes to an initial-SAT level and rebuilt the production-start criteria.

4M-based process recovery

Laser Welding Auto Cycle recovery

Process waiting conditions, sensing, alignment, and speed were corrected while safety interlocks remained unchanged.

Initial state

  • The equipment laser-welded the J/R uncoated area to the Collector.
  • Operators manually actuated the equipment from start to finish.
  • Manual intervention prevented normal MES process history.

4M cause map

4MProblem observedDecision and action
ManAbnormal reporting and cross-team action were disconnectedConnected owners and retests through interviews and a Punch List.
MachineSensor, setting, interlock, and speed mismatchReworked sensing, clamp, pressure, alignment, and equipment speed.
MaterialProduct geometry and reflectivity changes were not updatedRechecked detection against the latest product geometry.
MethodNo stable recovery and retest methodRedefined process purpose, tests, and close criteria.

Action

  • Engineering and maintenance jointly verified sensing and undocumented process interlocks.
  • Adjusted sensor position, clamp, pressure, alignment, and equipment speed.
  • Reduced only unnecessary unit wait time in product-detection and process-sequence logic.
  • Preserved door, laser, and operator safety interlocks.

Retest and gate

  • Used two 100-cell lots for Auto Cycle recovery tests.
  • Updated Punch items and retested them in the next lot.
  • Ran a final 50-cell one-way test.
  • Used FYD above 90% as the limited-production start criterion.

Result and control

  • Confirmed an Auto Cycle and decision flow sufficient to start limited production.
  • Revised work standards, QCP, checklists, and inspection criteria.
  • Established or updated P-FMEA, MES control items, and equipment Punch records.
  • This is not presented as long-term mass-production stabilization.
