Manufacturing Projects

Field application

2170 Assembly-Line Good-Yield Stabilization

Problem Equipment-reported yield and full-inspection good yield did not agree, so true process defects and inspection misclassification had to be separated under one basis.

Direct responsibility I aligned the yield denominator and measurement criteria, then managed owners, deadlines, local 4M+2E retests, and decision criteria on the same cells.

Result / current stage I contributed to the team result of stabilizing monthly average good yield from about 40% to 98.7% and directly revised the MSA criteria, SOP, QCP, P-FMEA, and inspection standards.
