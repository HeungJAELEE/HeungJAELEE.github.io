01 · MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing Field Projects

Cases that move from a measured field problem through cause analysis, equipment or process changes, retesting, and standardization.

Field application

Stabilizing 2170 Mass Production by Fixing Measurement First

Displayed yield, actual good yield, and shop-floor defects did not agree, so measurement and decision reliability had to be checked before process settings.

Field result / decision rule The team recorded 98.7% monthly average good yield in December 2024, and I directly revised the MSA criteria, SOP, QCP, P-FMEA, and inspection standards.

Production-transition validation

Revalidating 4695 Production Readiness from Initial SAT

Development and handover were complete, but no machine sustained a normal automatic cycle and vision inspection was inactive, so production depended on manual intervention.

Field result / decision rule The project confirmed an improvement from about 50% to about 95% good yield under full inspection and reached limited production to the quality standard.

Read case study
Trial production / qualification

25P LFP Power Cell Development, Trial Production, and Qualification

Material and structure changes in the LFP power cell made existing 2170 assembly and inspection settings unsuitable.

Field result / decision rule The team completed about 20,000-cell initial trial production, customer delivery, and qualification. Follow-on production did not proceed for commercial reasons.

Read case study
Concept validation

Manufacturing Concept Validation for a 2170 Tabless Cell

A collector-based Tabless structure differed from the lead-tab process and could not be repeated without dedicated jigs and inspection methods.

Field result / decision rule Five working samples charged and formed normally; a limited same-material, same-cycle comparison showed lower IR and improved life.

Pilot / validation

FAT/SAT of 11 Core Machines for an NCM Prismatic Pilot Line

Individual auto cycles could not prove end-to-end flow, safety, repeatability, or pilot-production readiness.

Field result / decision rule Initial assembly yield was about 95% across roughly 1,000 cells over five days, including reworked cells that met quality criteria.

Field application

Vacuum-Robot Overhaul Standardization

Experience-dependent assembly and inspection created variation in work time and missed checks.

Field result / decision rule Rework and overtime were observed to decrease, while team monthly output increased from 1–2 to 4–5 units during the same period.

High-Temperature Anodizing Film Development for OLED Parts

Film cracking and particles recurred within warranty life under repeated high-temperature use.

Field result / decision rule The revised condition entered production and was monitored for about a year through the transition from legacy-condition parts.

Field application

Technical Qualification of CVD and PECVD Components

Stable internal results were insufficient when customer measurement methods and acceptance criteria differed.

Field result / decision rule Same-sample measurement correlation was established, and the technical qualification conditions were added to QCPs, run sheets, and inspection standards.

Failure case

Diffuser Ultrasonic-Stripping Scale-up Failure and Validation Gates

Lab stripping was possible, but overlapping ultrasonic zones damaged the surface and left roughness, diffusion, and reusability risks unresolved.

Field result / decision rule Normal operation was not achieved; the case established that stripping feasibility and product reusability must be judged separately.
