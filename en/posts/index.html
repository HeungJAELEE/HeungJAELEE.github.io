Manufacturing Projects

Process development, equipment validation, production stabilization, and standard-work projects.

Field application

2170 Assembly-Line Yield Stabilization

Problem Displayed yield, actual good yield, and shop-floor defects did not agree, so measurement and decision reliability had to be checked before process settings.

Direct responsibility I led assembly-process trials and cross-team coordination, narrowing causes from measurement and sensors through inspection, jigs, consumables, and finally recipes.

Result / current stage The team recorded 98.7% monthly average good yield in December 2024, and I directly revised the MSA criteria, SOP, QCP, P-FMEA, and inspection standards.

Training PoC

Practical Weld-Specimen Vision AI Inspection Project

Problem The initial model trained on public weld images fell to 15.84% recall on directly captured specimens containing soot, spatter, oxidation, reflection, and angle variation.

Direct responsibility I planned the experiment and evaluation order and compared domain gap, FN/FP cases, candidate architectures, coordinate scaling, and threshold trade-offs.
