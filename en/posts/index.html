01 · MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing Field Projects

Cases that move from a measured field problem through cause analysis, equipment or process changes, retesting, and standardization.

Field application

Stabilizing 2170 Mass Production by Fixing Measurement First

Displayed yield, actual good yield, and shop-floor defects did not agree, so measurement and decision reliability had to be checked before process settings.

Field result / decision rule The team recorded 98.7% monthly average good yield in December 2024, and I directly revised the MSA criteria, SOP, QCP, P-FMEA, and inspection standards.

Limited-production validation

Revalidating 4695 Production Readiness from Initial SAT

Development and handover were complete, but automatic cycles and vision inspection did not run normally, operators moved each unit manually, and MES history was not recorded correctly.

Field result / decision rule After recovery trials covering about 200 cells in two lots over roughly two weeks, the final 50-cell one-way run achieved FYD of at least 90%, which was used as the limited-production start gate.

Translating Material and Structure Changes into 25P LFP Process Conditions

Material and structure changes in the LFP power cell made existing 2170 assembly and inspection settings unsuitable.

Field result / decision rule The team advanced through internal A, B, and C samples, about 20,000-cell initial trial production, customer qualification, and CCB P.O approval. Commercial production did not proceed because of pricing.

Validating a 2170 Tabless A-Sample Build with Temporary Jigs

A collector-based Tabless structure differed from the lead-tab process and could not be repeated without dedicated jigs and inspection methods.

Field result / decision rule After about 100 exploratory builds, five samples were suitable for functional checks; a limited same-material, same-cycle comparison indicated lower IR and improved life direction.
