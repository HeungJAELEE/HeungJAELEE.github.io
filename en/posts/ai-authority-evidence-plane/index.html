Why AI proposals must be separated from execution

Finding relevant material and proposing work does not grant execution authority or prove safe completion. This page is not a completed execution-system case; it is a design note for evidence, scope, human approval, and stop conditions required before work.

Manufacturing-equipment inspection example

When a user asks for a proposed inspection order based on equipment symptoms, AI can find related sources and draft work. A person must verify that sources fit the actual request and that the tool, target, duration, and stop conditions are appropriate.

Five checks before work

Scope I defined directly

I documented a work specification linking agent purpose, tools, targets, and stop conditions with evidence, approval, and result-verification order. AI may find knowledge and draft work, but it does not grant authority or finalize outcomes.

Current stage

The current result defines evidence, work scope, human approval, stop, and result-verification order. Restricted execution, an authorization UI, and external state checks can extend it into an execution-oriented agent.
