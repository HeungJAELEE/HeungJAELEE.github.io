Project objective
Manufacturing, semiconductor, and battery knowledge accumulated with inconsistent terms and links, making evidence retrieval difficult.
Planning and build scope
I structured the Markdown/YAML vault and link-graph index, then built a CLI that synchronizes changed files to ChromaDB and retrieves/reranks candidates.
Team result / current stage
Markdown source management, incremental synchronization, candidate retrieval, and reranking are implemented. Source-location display, authorization, and repeated retrieval evaluation can extend it into an operating manufacturing-knowledge tool.
