Manufacturing project summary

Problem definition
At YMC's new-business start, the new model had no parts list, overhaul manuals, inspection manual, or precision-setting criteria.
Key role
I performed the full incoming-to-teaching flow and created manuals and training for disassembly, replacement, precision assembly, alignment, zero, leak, FARO checks, and teaching.
Key result
Initial YMC qualification and about five units of one model were followed by deeper repeat-overhaul, operator, parts, and field standards at Bontech.

The two companies are not combined into one result. YMC was the zero-to-one stage: a new model was disassembled and converted into parts lists, manuals, and initial qualification. Bontech extended that foundation into repeatable overhaul, parts and operator control, and field execution.

Why a standard system was needed

At the start of the new business, parts lists, overhaul order, torque, alignment, encoder-zero, and inspection criteria were not documented. Different work and setting sequences could produce different leak and precision results even after assembly appeared complete.

After external technology transfer, I disassembled normal used units, failed units, and customer-returned robots, measured parts, and documented assembly relationships. The standards were based on model structure and precision-setting principles rather than publishing a manufacturer name.

Actual work sequence

Customer VOC and incoming inspection
→ record appearance and failure state
→ disassemble arm, body, and base
→ clean and measure parts
→ inspect/replace bearings, belts, motors, encoders, and seals
→ precision-assemble base and body
→ helium leak check
→ assemble arm
→ torque, alignment, and encoder zero
→ quality and repeated-motion checks
→ loaded dry run
→ shipment inspection
→ site installation and teaching

Scope I performed directly

I conducted incoming inspection, disassembly, cleaning, measurement, parts-list creation, life-limited component replacement, precision assembly, torque/alignment/zero, helium leak, FARO precision measurement, repeatability, straightness and vibration checks, motion programs, teaching, loaded cycles, dry runs, manuals, checklists, training, OJT, and independent-work confirmation.

Boundary between YMC and Bontech

At YMC, I worked in a three-person team to create parts lists and overhaul/operation/inspection manuals and completed the full flow from incoming inspection to site teaching. Initial qualification was followed by shipment of about five units of one model. Longer-term orders and sales were not directly verified.

At Bontech, I deepened precision setting, parts-life and operator control, equipment-process knowledge, and repeated overhaul execution. Overseas work included installation, alignment, teaching, initial qualification, and handoff, described only at a sanitized level.

Outputs and publication boundary

The work left parts lists, incoming inspection forms, failure records, overhaul and inspection manuals, torque/alignment/zero sequences, leak and precision checks, cycle criteria, teaching procedures, and training materials.

The previous monthly-output claim has been removed because orders, materials, staffing, and other factors were not separated. Manufacturer, model, internal settings, leak units and limits, and customer information are also excluded.
