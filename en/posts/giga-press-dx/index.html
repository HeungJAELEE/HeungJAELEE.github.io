Factory-expansion problem the team addressed

Meeting a target output required reviewing process time, waiting and transfer, and buffers rather than only adding equipment. The team created a virtual-factory scenario to inspect production flow and bottleneck candidates before equipment installation.

Team system and responsibility split

flowchart LR A["Factory I/O events\nTEAM"] --> B["PLC and OPC UA\nTEAM·SHARED"] --> C["SCADA and SQLite\nTEAM·SHARED"] --> D["Process time, waiting, buffer, output\nMY SCOPE"] --> E["Expansion scenario\nMY SCOPE"] E -.-> F["AI and PLC control\nNOT IMPLEMENTED"]

Teammates implemented the detailed Factory I/O, ladder, and SCADA components. I planned the project objective, overall structure, and roles, wrote the final report, and defined what production-decision meaning should be assigned to team-system states.

No AI implementation code or reproducible runtime remains. The case therefore does not claim completed AI optimization or PLC closed-loop control.

Scenario review and current result

The ten-person team connected Factory I/O, PLC, SCADA, OPC UA, and SQLite and demonstrated a normal virtual production flow plus a target-output expansion scenario. A single time value without a fixed measurement condition is excluded from results. Real-plant use would still require measured cycle time and logistics, safety PLCs, manufacturer protocols, communication-loss and recovery tests, hardware-in-the-loop, and FAT/SAT criteria.
