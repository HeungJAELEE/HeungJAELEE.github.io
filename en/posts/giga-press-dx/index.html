Why a Digital Twin was needed

The project framed three commissioning problems: repeated physical try-out, design-to-equipment data mismatch, and chronic loss that is difficult to trace when live state is disconnected from the virtual model.

Scope boundary: the project did not model molten-metal filling, die thermal deformation, casting pressure, or live safety behavior. It focused on sequence and interface review before physical commissioning.

Simplified GIGA Press process

Because Factory I/O did not contain a live GIGA Press, the machining stage was represented by a CNC-like process, followed by defect routing, tray transfer, stacker loading, and release.

How far DQN was applied

The project reviewed a state–action–Q-value structure for rack, stacker, and equipment state. It did not complete live-data closed-loop control.

Verified result and next validation

The verified result is an educational team demo and report. The 50–75% setup reduction, 50% expansion-effort reduction, minimized MTTR, and zero hardware-damage risk were not live before/after measurements.

  1. Hardware-in-the-Loop with real I/O and a safety PLC
  2. Missing sensor, stale value, network loss, reconnect, and restart scenarios
  3. AI input quality, timestamp alignment, and recommendation invalidation
  4. PLC interlocks, approve/reject/rollback, and manual-operation priority
  5. Comparable debugging, mismatch, recovery, and retest measures
