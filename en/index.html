About

I trace field problems through equipment behavior and data, then connect the finding to quality standards and operating procedures.

I am Jaeheung Lee, a manufacturing engineer who has worked on the development, validation, and production stabilization of secondary-battery cell assembly processes.

Across 9 years and 4 months of manufacturing experience, I have supported pilot and production-line setup, equipment FAT/SAT, process-condition optimization, and quality-document systems for 2170/4695 cylindrical cells and NCM prismatic cells. One verified career case stabilized a 2170 pilot-line yield from 40% to 98.7% by tracing equipment-signal noise and process conditions.

I now connect that field experience with Python, PLCs, Vision AI, digital twins, and knowledge graphs. My focus is practical manufacturing DX: define a shop-floor problem clearly, test it with data and automation, and state the evidence and limitations without overstating production readiness.

Working principles

  1. Read process conditions and equipment signals together.
  2. Connect quality documentation with data traceability.
  3. Separate PoC validation scope from production readiness.
