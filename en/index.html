About

I verify equipment and process causes against the same criteria, then record confirmed conditions in work and inspection standards.

I am Jaeheung Lee, a process and manufacturing engineer with experience spanning semiconductor/display process parts, vacuum robots, and battery pilot and production lines.

During 9 years and 4 months of work, I handled process development, equipment FAT/SAT, production planning, defect and downtime reduction, and standard work. On a 2170 assembly line, I used MSA to separate inspection misclassification from actual process defects, then improved jig tolerances, recipes, wear parts, and inspection conditions under a 4M review. Good-product yield increased from about 40% to 98.7% within the recorded scope.

I now extend the same problem-definition and validation habits through Python, PLCs, Vision AI, and digital twins. Each public case states what I owned, how I checked it, what changed, and what remains unverified.

How I work in the field

  1. Verify the inspection method and equipment condition before trusting a measurement.
  2. Change one variable at a time and retest against the same criteria.
  3. Record confirmed conditions in work standards, inspection criteria, QCP, and P-FMEA.
