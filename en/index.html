About

My experience covers process development, equipment acceptance, and production stabilization.

I studied materials science and spent nine years and four months in manufacturing, from semiconductor and display process parts to vacuum robots and battery pilot and production lines.

When starting a line or investigating a process problem, I first check the yield definition, acceptance criteria, measurement method, and equipment condition. I then separate measurement variation from actual process defects and retest one condition at a time under the same criteria. Confirmed settings go into SOPs, QCP, P-FMEA, and inspection standards so the next operator can make the same decision.

I now use Python, PLCs, and Vision AI to automate recurring manufacturing checks and data reviews.

How I solve a process problem

  1. Check the measurement method and equipment condition first.
  2. Separate causes and retest one condition at a time.
  3. Record confirmed settings in work and inspection standards.
