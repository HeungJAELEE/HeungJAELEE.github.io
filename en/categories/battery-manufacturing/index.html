Field application

2170 Assembly-Line Yield Stabilization

Problem Displayed yield, actual good yield, and shop-floor defects did not agree, so measurement and decision reliability had to be checked before process settings.

Direct responsibility I led assembly-process trials and cross-team coordination, narrowing causes from measurement and sensors through inspection, jigs, consumables, and finally recipes.

Result / current stage The team recorded 98.7% monthly average good yield in December 2024, and I directly revised the MSA criteria, SOP, QCP, P-FMEA, and inspection standards.

Pilot / validation

NCM Prismatic Pilot-Line Acceptance and Initial Stabilization

Problem Limited FAT samples and individual auto cycles could not prove end-to-end flow, repeatability, or readiness for actual production.

Direct responsibility I built acceptance sheets and punch lists, tracked supplier corrections and retests, and led actual cell production plus measurement and process-standard work.

Result / current stage The team observed an initial snapshot of about 95% over roughly five days and 1,000 cells, including cells meeting criteria after rework, and documented FAT/SAT, MSA, and operating standards. It is not FPY or long-term production yield.
