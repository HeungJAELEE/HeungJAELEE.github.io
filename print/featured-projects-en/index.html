Problem

PeriodMay-Nov 2023
Direct responsibilityFAT/SAT · acceptance criteria · material-specific jigs · operating standards
StagePilot validation

Problem

Supplier sign-off did not prove that the line was ready for pilot production. After pre-welding, positive-tab foil tearing, negative-electrode coating loss, and shorts recurred, while manual forming variation obscured the cause.

What I did

  • Set acceptance criteria for 11 systems, 13 processes, and the separate EOL check
  • Closed FAT/SAT gaps through punch-list retests using actual cell-quality criteria
  • Used 4M to separate Al-foil tearing from heavy-edge coating loss and designed material-specific pilot jigs
  • Screened ~200 samples, verified 4 lots×50, and transferred controls to standards and the production-equipment RFQ

Problem

Problem

Finding relevant documents and drafting an action does not give AI execution authority or guarantee a safe stop and verified completion. Evidence version, approval owner, allowed tools, failure conditions, and authoritative result readback must be connected in one operating procedure.

  • Separated request, external execution, and authoritative readback

Weld Defect Disposition Vision AI PoC

Separated public training data from field-like images and defined missed-defect and manual-review controls in a training PoC

Team5-person training PoC
Direct responsibilityOverall planning · AI modeling · evaluation rules · missed-defect and manual-review gates
StageTraining PoC · before production integration

Problem

Performance on public weld images alone could not establish fitness for production inspection. Field-like images differed in soot, spatter, metal reflection, lighting, and camera angle, while automatically accepting low-confidence defects could create a quality escape.

What I did

  • Separated ~30k undercut, ~30k overlap, and ~198 field-like images by purpose
  • Applied preprocessing/augmentation; compared ResNet and DeiT candidates
  • Used score, threshold, and confusion matrices to evaluate FN and FP
  • Routed low confidence to manual review; set independent-test, lot, signal, cycle, and recovery gates
